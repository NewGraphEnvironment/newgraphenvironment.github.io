## Mechanism

Each quantified subject was written from the current member (the 2025 report, the catalogue as built, the tool's showcase example). When a finding narrowed it, only the predicate that tripped the finding was checked. The enumeration found quantifier words sentence by sentence. It therefore misses three things: predicates that inherit a subject's quantifier across a sentence boundary ("Each ... project since 2020 is ... It carries ..."); a fix that swaps one universal for a synonym ("every" for "each") and leaves the scope where it was; and universals carried by a relative clause or a colon rather than a quantifier word ("lists what exists", "so the record keeps growing: ... back to 2002"). The R2 fixes themselves hold: Since 2020 all 20 linked reports carry a version and date on the title page (checked on the live sites), all 20 repos are public, and the three sampled have a `CITATION.cff`.
