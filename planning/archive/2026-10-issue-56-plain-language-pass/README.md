It also set a copy rule: celebrate what we have, with no roadmap wording, and state a caveat a reader needs as a present-tense fact.

What was learned repeats #53 in a sharper form. Higher-level wording drops qualifiers faster than detailed wording does. The plan review and three code-check rounds found 30 claim defects, two of them inside earlier fixes. Round 3 named the mechanism: a qualifier stated once in a source (at stations, thumbnails, where published, an index not a copy, not valid) does not travel with its subject to the next sentence, table row or page that names it. The loop ended by enumeration: all 88 changed claims (round 2), then every mention of 15 qualified subjects (round 3).
