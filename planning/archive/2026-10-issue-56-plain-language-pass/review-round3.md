## Mechanism

**The copy assumes that a qualifier stated once stays attached to its subject wherever the subject appears again.** Examples are "at stations", "thumbnails", "where published", "an index, not a copy" and "not valid yet". The source puts each qualifier in one sentence. The page then names the same subject again in a neighbouring sentence, a table row, a table header, a heading or the landing page, and the new place carries no qualifier, because the writer counts on the nearby one. A reader meets each of those places on its own, and the landing page and the table are where readers skim. So when a fix corrects the one sentence a reviewer quoted, the claim survives in the next place that names the same subject. That happened with "Georeferenced photos" (fly thumbnails, round 1 then round 2). It happened with the "the data" sentence and the "What it holds" table header below it (round 2, then this round).

So the places this mechanism can reach are all the places that name a subject whose source attaches a scope. I listed them by subject, not by sentence.

## Findings

- **F1 [overstated; claim moved from the fixed sentence into the table header]** OSS:198 `| Catalogue | What it holds |` with OSS:200 `| stac_dem_bc | LiDAR elevation models, bare earth and surface; province-wide, updated monthly |`.
  - ba46656 changed OSS:192 from "the data" to "the catalogue itself" because the lidar catalogues do not hold LidarBC's files. The table six lines below still says, per row, what each catalogue *holds*. The header was renamed from "What it catalogues" to "What it holds" on this branch, in `a4bc36d` (the base `9d0822fb` line 204 reads "What it catalogues").
  - Row 201 adds "(an index, not a copy)" for point clouds. Row 200 adds nothing, so by contrast the reader infers that stac_dem_bc does hold copies of the elevation models.
  - Source: stac_dem_bc README: "serves British Columbia's LidarBC elevation data…**102,460 tiles on the provincial objectstore**". Live item `094-094o-2026-dem-bc_094o056_2_1_4_xli1m_utm10_20260506_20260506`: `dem` → `https://nrs.objectstore.gov.bc.ca/gdwuts/094/094o/2026/dem/…tif`, `dsm` → `https://nrs.objectstore.gov.bc.ca/gdwuts/094/094o/2026/dsm/…_dsm.tif`.
  - The same row also drops the DSM qualifier that OSS:152 carries. stac_dem_bc README: "`dsm` digital surface model, **where the delivery published one — 95,888 of 102,460**".
  - Suggested fix: restore the header to "What it catalogues", which fixes every row at once. Make row 200 "LidarBC elevation models, bare earth and, where published, surface; province-wide, updated monthly". Optionally, give row 200 the same "(an index, not a copy)" as row 201, for parallelism.

- **F2 [qualifier dropped, minor/borderline; author decision]** OSS:50 "At gauging stations it adds daily flow and water temperature." This scope also governs OSS:52 "…and water temperature with the recent record" (the ba46656 line) and LND:336 "water temperature at gauging stations".
  - Source: wet README:15 "Daily water temperature from the ECCC readings that water-temp-bc archives (**about 300 stations**, from 2002)". water-temp-bc README "What's in it": parameter 5, water temperature, **306 stations**. By contrast, wet's daily flow (README:14) comes from HYDAT for any station (`wet_station_daily("08EE013")`). So temperature exists at a subset of the gauging stations that have daily flow.
  - The page applies exactly this kind of subset qualifier to surface models ("where published", OSS:152) but not here.
  - Round 2 held these lines, on the grounds that they do not say "every". Under the mechanism they are the same shape as F1's DSM row, so I am recording them for a decision rather than as a defect.
  - Suggested fix, if wanted: OSS:50 "At gauging stations it adds daily flow and, where the station logs it, water temperature." LND:336 "water temperature at the gauging stations that log it". OSS:52 then inherits the scope from OSS:50.
